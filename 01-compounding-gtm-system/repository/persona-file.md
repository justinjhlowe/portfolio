# Persona File — Ledgerly

**Version:** 4 | **Last updated:** week 4 | **Linked:** `positioning-file.md`, `icp-file.md`

## Controller (primary buyer's right hand, day-to-day owner)

**Day to day:** Owns the close. Chases approvals in email and Slack threads that don't connect to anything. Reconciles AP manually against the ERP because the AP tool, if there is one, doesn't talk to NetSuite cleanly.

**Top pains, in their words (pulled from discovery calls and G2 reviews):**
1. "We close three days late every month because invoice approvals sit in someone's inbox."
2. "I find out about a duplicate payment after it's already gone out."
3. "Audit season means two weeks of pulling backup documentation by hand."

**Trigger that moves them to act:** A bad audit finding, a new CFO asking why close takes so long, or hitting a transaction volume where the manual process visibly breaks (usually around 500+ invoices/month).

**Objections:**
- "We already have a BPO handling overflow AP." (see `competitor-radar.md` — BPO is now tracked as a fourth alternative, not just software competitors)
- "Implementation will blow up our close calendar for a quarter." (countered with phased rollout proof point)

**What they trust:** A peer reference from a Controller at a similar-size company more than any deck. Wants to see the audit trail feature live, not described.

**Messaging pillar that lands:** Audit-ready by default (Pillar 2 in positioning file — promoted to primary in v3 after reply-rate data, see change history).

## VP Finance / CFO (economic buyer)

**Day to day:** Owns the budget and answers to the board on close speed and controls. Doesn't touch AP day to day but owns the consequence of it being broken.

**Top pains:**
1. "I can't tell the board we're scaling if closing the books takes longer every quarter."
2. "I don't want to add headcount to solve a process problem."
3. "Our last audit took longer than it should have and I don't want a repeat."

**Trigger:** Board pressure on efficiency metrics, or a funding round that puts finance operations under new scrutiny.

**Objections:**
- "Show me the ROI, not the features."
- "What's the switching cost if this doesn't work out."

**What they trust:** ROI math tied to close-time reduction and headcount avoided, not feature comparisons.

**Messaging pillar that lands:** No new headcount to scale AP volume (Pillar 3).

## AP Manager / Specialist (influencer, sometimes blocker)

**Day to day:** Runs point on the actual invoice queue. Often the person whose job description changes most if this gets bought.

**Top pains:**
1. "I'm doing data entry, not analysis."
2. Worried the tool replaces them rather than upgrades them.

**Trigger:** Rarely initiates. Becomes an ally once framed as "fewer manual entries, more oversight" rather than headcount reduction.

**Objections:** Quiet resistance more than vocal objection. Needs to hear this from the Controller, not from us directly.

**What they trust:** Peer-level walkthroughs, not sales messaging.

**Messaging pillar that lands:** N/A — this persona is managed through the Controller relationship, not targeted directly in outbound.
